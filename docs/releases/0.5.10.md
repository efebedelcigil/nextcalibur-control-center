# Nextcalibur 0.5.10

Two things found by taking 0.5.9 as an update on the machine.

## Fixed

- **One entry in *Installed apps*.** A copy that updated from 0.5.8 showed
  Nextcalibur twice: 0.5.9 repaired the broken entry 0.5.7 and 0.5.8 had
  left, and the updater added one of its own beside it. The duplicate goes
  on the first start of 0.5.10, and after every update from now on.
- **"Check for updates now" says it is checking.** The window comes up with
  a progress bar while the check runs, and then the answer - up to date, or
  the update on offer. Until now nothing showed until the answer came.
