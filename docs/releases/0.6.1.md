# Nextcalibur 0.6.1

A hotfix for 0.6.0's cool-down notification.

## Fixed

- **"Cool down before it gets hot" spoke too soon.** With the window open
  the temperature is read every five seconds, and the rule "heading for the
  warning line on two readings in a row" was five seconds of it: the burst
  of work when the application starts was enough to suggest Fn+1 for
  nothing. The climb now has to hold for at least thirty seconds as well.
