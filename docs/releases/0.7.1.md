# Nextcalibur 0.7.1

Fixes for what 0.7.0 got wrong on a real machine, and settings kept across
what Windows does while Nextcalibur is closed.

## Fixed

- **The processor ceilings could not be chosen in Turkish** (and other Settings
  controls stayed disabled at a start): the Settings page was built before the
  machine's own check had finished, and never again. Switching the language
  happened to rebuild it, so English worked. Built again once the check is done.
- **Games found by the daily scan never switched to Gaming**: the scan filled
  the list but left the program rules off. Games found now switch the rules
  on, and say so - unless you turned them off yourself.
- **Balanced was missing from two lists of the modes**: its tab stayed selected
  on a plan that is none of the modes', and `nextcalibur plans` skipped it.

## New

- **Your screen-off and sleep times are kept.** A change you make in Windows
  while Nextcalibur runs is yours. One found at a start - made while it was
  closed, by a Windows update most often - is said, with a "Put my times back"
  button; nothing is written by itself.
- **Processor ceilings put back at a start are now said** (they always were put back).
